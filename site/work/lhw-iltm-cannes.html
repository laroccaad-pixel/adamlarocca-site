Leading Hotels of the World (LHW) is the world’s largest collection of exclusive, independent luxury hotels, each property representing the highest standard of service and hospitality.

International Luxury Trade Market Cannes is the premier annual gathering of the global luxury travel industry.

To reinforce LHW’s position as the definitive leader in luxury, we were challenged to create a full signage takeover of the event. The solution centered on elegantly curated imagery and a bold, brand-forward identity, maintaining impeccable consistency while showcasing the scale, diversity, and prestige of LHW’s portfolio.
