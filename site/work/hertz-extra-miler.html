Hertz unseated Enterprise as #1 in the J.D. Power customer satisfaction ranking in car rental for the first time ever.

They wanted to highlight their achievement with a brand campaign letting their customers know that they were the inspiration behind it all.

Extra Miler Campaign

Times Square Takeover

Hertz’s return to the NASDAQ was a defining brand moment. To announce its re-IPO and the acquisition of the world’s largest EV fleet, I designed a ten-screen Times Square system engineered to work as a single, cohesive experience across different formats and sightlines. The takeover cut through the visual noise of Times Square while reinforcing Hertz’s bold, modern brand presence at a critical point in its comeback.
