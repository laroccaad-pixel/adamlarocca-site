Daytona Beach has long been known as a spring break party destination.

But the brand set out to redefine itself as an easy, welcoming place for families. Knowing the beach was the undeniable hero, we created a rallying cry that reestablished Daytona Beach as the authority on beach fun and the ultimate family beach vacation, grounded in the carefree memories made there.

Times Square Anamorphic Billboard

Daytona Beach is a favorite for local weekenders, but longer trips from out-of-state visitors needed a boost. To shift perception, we created an anamorphic billboard in Times Square, spotlighting new direct flights from NYC and showing how easy it is to trade cold for “Beach On” energy.
