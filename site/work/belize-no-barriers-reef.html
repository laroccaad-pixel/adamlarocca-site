Crystal-clear waters and a thriving marine ecosystem have made the Belize Barrier Reef one of the world’s most celebrated dive destinations.

Each year, travelers experience its beauty firsthand. But for many Belizeans living closest to it, physical barriers have kept the reef out of reach.

This film captured the moment those barriers fell. By bringing the reef to Belizeans with physical disabilities through immersive VR, it transformed access into emotion, creating moments of connection, gratitude, and inclusion. The film became a catalyst for conversation around accessibility and conservation.

Social Teasers & Influencers

As World Oceans Day approached, we teased the emotional impact of the immersive experience. We then partnered with Belizean and accessibility-focused influencers to drive awareness around the launch.

Explore the Reef in VR / 360°

We brought the 360° underwater journey to audiences online and through live events, allowing anyone to explore the beauty of the world’s largest living reef.
