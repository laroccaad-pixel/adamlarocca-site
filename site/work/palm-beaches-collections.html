The Palm Beaches is one of the most diverse destinations in the country, spanning 39 cities and offering everything from family travel to wellness, nature, and luxury.

We turned that diversity into the idea itself, inviting travelers to curate their own “collection” and build a vacation tailored to them.

To break through a category full of predictable beach imagery, we introduced a distinctive tablescape system. Elevated, tactile, and modular, it allowed experiences, activities, and moments to be artfully arranged and recombined, signaling both variety and refinement at a glance. Built around key brand pillars, the campaign flexed seamlessly across audiences and individual cities, while maintaining a consistent visual language across all media.
