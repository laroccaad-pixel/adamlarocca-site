Thrifty Car Rental has always delivered the right balance of value and quality, but few people knew it.

To drive awareness and consideration, we launched Thrifty’s first TV campaign in over a decade, reimagining a familiar story with a bit of humorswagg™. Starring Kenan Thompson as Goldilocks, the work proved that “just right” can still stand out.
