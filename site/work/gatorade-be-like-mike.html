Everyone wanted to
Be Like Mike.

So as the 23-year anniversary of Gatorade’s most iconic campaign approached, we saw an opportunity not to recreate it, but to reintroduce it to a new generation.

Today’s teen athletes don’t just say who inspires them, they show it. Using #BeLikeMike, they turned a legendary idea into something personal, social, and alive again. The result helped make this the most viewed campaign in Gatorade’s history.

NBA All‑Star Event

We launched #BeLikeMike with a live event during NBA All-Star game weekend, transporting teens back in time and allowing them to recreate Michael Jordan’s most iconic moments.

Social

The love for #23 poured in, as amateur and professional athletes took to social, showing us, in their own way, how they #BeLikeMike.

17 posts. Swipe or use the arrows.

Capstone Video

In the end, we curated their best submissions into a capstone piece set to the original commercial’s song, proving we all still want to Be Like Mike.

ESPN HPTO

We worked with ESPN to build a custom homepage takeover, launching their new site redesign while celebrating one of MJ's most iconic moments.
